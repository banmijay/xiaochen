隐私权政策

  • 小晨只供本人使用，只读取本人授权的 Google 账号数据（邮件只读）。
  • 不发送、不删除、不修改任何邮件。
  • 数据只在本人的电脑上处理和保存，不出售、不分享给任何第三方。
  • 可以随时在 Google 账户的「第三方应用和服务」里撤销授权。

Privacy Policy

  • Xiaochen is used only by its owner and only reads data from Google accounts the owner has authorized (read-only email access).
  • It does not send, delete or modify any email.
  • Data is processed and stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time under "Third-party apps & services" in the Google Account.
